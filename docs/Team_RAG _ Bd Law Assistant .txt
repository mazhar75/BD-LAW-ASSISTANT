Project Proposal: BD Law Assistant (RAG-Based Legal AI System)
1. Project Overview
BD Law Assistant is an AI-driven legal research and assistance platform designed to empower individuals, law firms, and legal aid organizations in Bangladesh by delivering rapid, contextually accurate answers to legal queries in Bengali. Leveraging Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and state-of-the-art Large Language Models (LLMs), this solution simplifies access to case laws, statutes, and legal interpretations, reducing reliance on time-consuming manual research.
2. Problem Statement
Access to Bangladeshi legal documents is often fragmented across multiple sources, making comprehensive research labor-intensive and error-prone. Non-lawyers face challenges interpreting complex legal language, while small firms and aid organizations have limited resources for dedicated legal research staff.
3. Proposed Solution
BD Law Assistant will integrate semantic retrieval and generative AI to:
* Retrieve relevant legal documents and excerpts from a structured vector database.
* Generate concise, context-aware answers in Bengali that reference authoritative legal sources.
* Summarize lengthy judgments, statutes, and regulations into digestible bullet points for quick comprehension.
* Support conversational interactions via a chatbot interface for follow-up queries.

* Frontend: React SPA with intuitive search bar and Q&A chat interface.
* API Gateway: Java Spring Boot handling authentication, request routing, and logging.
* RAG Microservice: Python service using LangChain to orchestrate embedding creation, vector retrieval, and LLM-driven generation.
* Vector Store: FAISS/Pinecone/Weaviate for high-performance semantic search of preprocessed legal text segments.
5. Technology Stack
* Frontend: React.js, TypeScript, Redux, Tailwind CSS
* Backend: Java Spring Boot, Spring Security, Hibernate (REST API)
* AI Service: Python 3.10+, LangChain, Transformers (Hugging Face), OpenAI API (or self-hosted LLMs)
* Vector Database: FAISS (open source) or Pinecone/Weaviate
* Data Storage: PostgreSQL (metadata), AWS S3 (document storage)
* Deployment & DevOps: Docker, Kubernetes, GitHub Actions, AWS/GCP/Azure
6. Business Use Cases
* General Public: Instant, layman-friendly legal guidance on routine matters (e.g., tenancy rights, contract queries).
* Law Firms: Accelerated case research and precedent identification.
* Legal Aid NGOs: Streamlined support workflows and reduced case preparation time.
* Government Portals: Augmented public access to legal information and e-governance services.
7. Technical Use Cases
* Semantic Legal Search: Retrieve precise passages from judgments and statutes.
* Automated Summarization: Condense lengthy rulings into key points.
* Conversational Q&A: Multi-turn dialogue handling for in-depth research.
* Document Ingestion Pipeline: Scalable preprocessing (cleaning, chunking, embedding) of new legal documents.
